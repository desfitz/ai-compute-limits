## Method: What a Fermi Estimate Can and Cannot Say
*Build the answer from many bounded factors; the few loose ones carry the uncertainty, and those are the only ones worth arguing about.*

  > A Fermi estimate multiplies many bounded factors; errors add in quadrature, so the few loose factors dominate.

  > The paper therefore decomposes the loose inputs and reports which two or three carry the doubt.

## The Supply-Side Carry: A Vintage Model
*Each year's spending stays on the books for years, so the yearly cost keeps climbing even if spending stops growing.*
In brief:
  > Each year's spending is a cohort that stays on the books: chips for about four years, buildings for about twenty.
  > The yearly cost of the whole stack - wear, running costs and a return on the money - is the carry.
  > On flat spending the carry climbs from about 390B in 2027 to about 725B in 2030, because old cohorts are still being paid for as new ones arrive.
  > AI chips run hot and wear out faster than the accounting lives assume, and they cannot be cheaply reused.
